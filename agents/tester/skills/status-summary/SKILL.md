# Status Summary

Summarize the state of the work you are on in **3-5 bullets**:

- **Done:** finished and verified work
- **Left:** the concrete next steps
- **Blocked:** a decision, permission or missing piece of information you are waiting on (write "no blockers" if none)

Keep it short; no filler, every bullet points to a concrete piece of work.
If you are not working on anything, say so plainly; do not invent work.
